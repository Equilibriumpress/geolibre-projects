# Population density in Utrecht

Which Utrecht neighbourhoods have the highest population density?

Publication: [111111111111](https://github.com/Equilibriumpress/geolibre-projects/commit/1111111111111111111111111111111111111111) · App build: 6f19272f2bd1. Project files use the linked publication revision.

Data year: 2025 · Utrecht, Netherlands · Updated: 2026-10-04

## Reading the result

The map ranks population density within Utrecht. A score of 100 identifies the highest density, not a judgement of social need. Selection shows the original residents/km² value.

- Neighbourhood boundaries aggregate different land uses. A neighbourhood average does not describe every street.
- The project reads live CBS/PDOK data. Later source revisions can change the ranks.

## Method & provenance

**Analysis unit:** CBS neighbourhoods  
**Analysis year:** 2025  
**Indicators:** 1

| Indicator | Unit | Year | Direction |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Population density | residents/km² | 2025 | higher-is-worse |

SQL and runtime use average ranks for ties. Invalid values remain missing. Available weights determine the score, while confidence reports weighted data coverage.

**Policy scenarios**

- Population density
