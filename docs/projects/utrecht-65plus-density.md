# Ageing and population density in Utrecht

Which Utrecht neighbourhoods combine a high share of residents aged 65+ with high population density?

Publication: [111111111111](https://github.com/Equilibriumpress/geolibre-projects/commit/1111111111111111111111111111111111111111) · App build: 6f19272f2bd1. Project files use the linked publication revision.

Data year: 2025 · Utrecht, Netherlands · Updated: 2026-10-04

## Reading the result

The index averages the two within-Utrecht percentile ranks with equal weights. A high mean indicates a high combined rank. One high component also raises the mean, so inspect both values in the dashboard.

- This is a descriptive index, not a health-risk or care-demand estimate.
- Live source updates can change ranks. The geographic filter and missing-value rules affect the comparison.

## Method & provenance

**Analysis unit:** CBS neighbourhoods  
**Analysis year:** 2025  
**Indicators:** 2

| Indicator | Unit | Year | Direction |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Population density | residents/km² | 2025 | higher-is-worse |
| Residents aged 65+ | % | 2025 | higher-is-worse |

SQL and runtime use average ranks for ties. Invalid values remain missing. Available weights determine the score, while confidence reports weighted data coverage.

**Policy scenarios**

- Ageing + density
