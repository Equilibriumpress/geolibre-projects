# Rotterdam Investment Priority Lab

Which Rotterdam grid cells remain high priorities across different public-investment perspectives?

Publication: [111111111111](https://github.com/Equilibriumpress/geolibre-projects/commit/1111111111111111111111111111111111111111) · App build: 6f19272f2bd1. Project files use the linked publication revision.

Data year: 2024 · Rotterdam, Netherlands · Updated: 2026-10-04

## Reading the result

Compare five policy scenarios on the official CBS 500 metre grid. Scores are relative to the loaded urban-core extent. Confidence describes available weighted data separately from priority.

- Scenario weights express policy choices, not an objectively correct funding ranking.
- The pilot covers the urban core, not the whole municipality.

## Method & provenance

**Analysis unit:** CBS 500 x 500 metre statistical grid  
**Analysis year:** 2024  
**Indicators:** 11

| Indicator | Unit | Year | Direction |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Population pressure | residents | 2024 | higher-is-worse |
| Residents aged 65+ | % | 2024 | higher-is-worse |
| Residents aged 0-14 | % | 2024 | higher-is-worse |
| Low-income households | % | 2024 | higher-is-worse |
| Residents receiving benefits | % | 2024 | higher-is-worse |
| Single-parent households | % | 2024 | higher-is-worse |
| Average household income | CBS income unit | 2024 | lower-is-worse |
| Distance to GP practice | km | 2024 | higher-is-worse |
| Distance to primary school | km | 2024 | higher-is-worse |
| Distance to large supermarket | km | 2024 | higher-is-worse |
| Distance to train station | km | 2024 | higher-is-worse |

SQL and runtime use average ranks for ties. Invalid values remain missing. Available weights determine the score, while confidence reports weighted data coverage.

**Policy scenarios**

- Balanced investment
- Healthy ageing
- Family access
- Social vulnerability
- Service pressure
