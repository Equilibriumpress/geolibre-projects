# Video request

- Goal: Show one coherent Norwegian camper route as a standalone visual story.
- Audience: Travellers planning a scenic Norway road trip.
- Platform/preset: TikTok / Reel / Short
- Target duration: about 30 seconds.
- Route: Norwegian Scenic Route Geiranger–Trollstigen.
- Opening: Full-screen Trollstigen visual hook → Western Norway → complete route.
- Route movement: geometry-driven 3D route follow using `norway-camper-route`.
- Photo stops: Flydalsjuvet, Ørnesvingen, Gudbrandsjuvet, Trollstigen.
- Text: concise location and route context on the hook, overview and photo stops. No credit overlays.
- Transitions: soft photo crossfades and camera fly transitions. Never reveal the underlying map before the opening image or after the closing image.
- Closing: hold Trollstigen long enough to read “Would you drive this?”.
- Narration: no.
- Audio notes: unchanged.

All route facts and images must remain traceable through `sources.json`.
